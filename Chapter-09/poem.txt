Twinkle, twinkle, little star,
Shining brightly from afar,
Guiding travelers through the night,
With your gentle, silver light.

When the blazing sun is gone,
And the daylight has withdrawn,
Still you sparkle, pure and true,
Painting skies with diamond hue.

High above the world you stay,
Watching children as they play,
Tiny lantern in the sky,
Glowing softly, never shy.

Through the darkness, clear and deep,
You awaken while we sleep,
Silent guardian overhead,
Keeping watch where dreams are spread.

Though so small and far away,
Still you brighten every day,
Twinkle, twinkle, little star,
How I wonder what you are!
