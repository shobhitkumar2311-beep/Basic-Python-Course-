The donkey, often underestimated, is a symbol of patience and hard work. Physically smaller than horses, donkeys possess remarkable strength and endurance, making them valuable in rural communities for carrying loads and plowing fields. Socially, they are affectionate animals that form strong bonds thea humans and other donkeys. Economically, they remain crucial in many developing regions where machines are scarce. Culturally, the donkey represents humility and persistence, appearing in stories and traditions worldwide. Despite its modest appearance, the donkey’s intelligence and loyalty make it far more than a simple beast of burden.
